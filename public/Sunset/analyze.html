Content Analysis

The GoArmy winter-sports page presents a compelling narrative that combines historical content with contemporary recruitment messaging. Key observations:

Content Structure:

Key Insight: Approximately 70-80% of the page content is historically evergreen and remains valuable long after the 2026 Winter Olympics conclude. The historical narrative, Army legacy, and general recruitment messaging have lasting relevance.

Recommendation

Convert URL into Evergreen Hub

Rationale

This page is an ideal candidate for conversion to an evergreen hub. The content demonstrates high relevancy beyond the campaign period, with substantial historical and educational value that remains valuable year-round. The combination of high page value, occasional sunset frequency (4-year cycle), and high content relevancy makes investing in a hub conversion the optimal strategy.

Implementation Steps

  • Keep the current URL (/winter-sports) - maintain SEO equity and backlinks
  • Remove time-sensitive elements: Update "2026 Winter Sports" and "The Return to Italy" sections to be more evergreen or archive them
  • Apply "Evergreen Hub Template": Restructure to emphasize historical content and ongoing Army World Class Athlete Program
  • Update copy and CTAs: Make recruitment messaging more general (not Olympics-specific)
  • Add evergreen content: Expand historical sections, add more about current WCAP athletes, include year-round Army sports programs
  • Maintain in navigation: Keep accessible in main site navigation as "Army Winter Sports" or "Army Sports History"
  • Future-proof: Create a template that can easily incorporate future Olympic campaigns without requiring full page rebuild

Expected Benefits

  • Preserve SEO value: Maintain high-value backlinks and organic traffic
  • Reduce maintenance: Avoid rebuilding page every 4 years for Olympics
  • Year-round value: Historical content attracts traffic throughout the year
  • Recruitment continuity: Maintain recruitment messaging without campaign gaps
  • Content scalability: Easy to add new Olympic campaigns as they occur

Alternative Consideration

If resources are constrained, consider a hybrid approach: Keep the historical content evergreen while creating a separate, campaign-specific section that can be updated every 4 years. This maintains the hub value while allowing for fresh Olympic content.

Key Takeaways

  • ~85-90% of content stays the same: Historical narrative, biographies, program info, and CTAs remain unchanged
  • Simple word swaps: "2026" → "Army", "This year" → "Today", remove location-specific references

Content Relevancy Analysis

Why High Relevancy? The page's content structure is approximately 70-80% evergreen:

  • Historical narrative (40%): 10th Mountain Division history, WWII impact, founding of ski resorts - permanently valuable
  • Biographical content (20%): Profiles of historical figures remain relevant for educational and historical research
  • Modern program info (15%): World Class Athlete Program is ongoing, not campaign-specific
  • Recruitment messaging (15%): General Army recruitment CTAs remain relevant year-round
  • Campaign-specific (10%): Only "2026 Winter Sports" and "Return to Italy" sections are time-bound

This high relevancy ratio, combined with the occasional sunset frequency (4-year cycle), strongly supports converting to an evergreen hub rather than archiving or redirecting.
