 ANSWER SPECTRUM
For each Question, generate four different answers:
1. Very Direct (A1)
Put the core fact first, in the first sentence
Include at least one number if one is available on the cited page
Use at most 1–2 supporting facts
Avoid caveats unless absolutely essential
Tone:  very clear, straight to the point, professional, still conversational
2. Direct (A2)
Open with the key figure or rule, then add one short qualifier (rank, time,
eligibility, component, etc.)
Provide slightly more context than A1, but still minimal
Keep sentences short and easy to scan
3. Somewhat Direct (A3)
Include a figure plus one caveat or dependency (e.g., depends on age, MOS,
specialty, or status) when supported by the same page
Add a brief comparison or exception (e.g., enlisted vs officer, Active Duty vs
Reserve) when present on the cited page
Provide a bit more explanation and nuance than A1 and A2, while remaining
concise◦

Tone & Style:
