Deduplication Rules:
Deduplicate by intent AND phrasing :
No near duplicates or minor rewrites
Each question should either: 
Ask about a different aspect of the topic, OR
Ask from a clearly different angle (e.g., skeptical vs curious), OR
T arget a different persona's concern
Questions with the same intent but different wording are still duplicates
Examples of duplicates to avoid:
 "How long is basic training?" vs "What's the length of basic training?"
 "Do I need a high school diploma?" vs "Is a diploma required to join?"
Examples of acceptable variations:
 "How long is basic training?" (Neutral, general)
 "I'm worried basic training might be too hard for me. How physically
demanding is it?" (Skeptical, specific angle)
